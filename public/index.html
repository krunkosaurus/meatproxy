How it works

Open a link to receive files automatically. Keep both tabs open until the transfer finishes, then save your files.

Files and notes are encrypted. They travel directly when possible, or through an encrypted relay. Files are never stored on our server.

Anyone with the complete link can receive. One recipient at a time, up to 100 files and 256 MB. Links expire when the sender leaves or after 12 hours.
